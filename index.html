  • Discover curated North Washington craft selections
  • Community leaderboards, tasting wall & shared notes

CastWA

Washington fishing — every spot, every rule, at your fingertips.

Washington State has some of the best fishing in North America, and some of the most complex regulations to match. CastWA puts the whole picture in your pocket — find productive spots, check current conditions, and stay legal — so you spend less time in the rulebook and more time on the water.

  • Interactive map of fishing spots statewide
  • WDFW regulations, seasons & limits — always current
  • Species guides for 50+ Washington fish & shellfish

About the Studio

Small studio.
No shortcuts.

Z-Plot Industries is one developer — Zach Phillips — building apps from the Pacific Northwest with an obsessive focus on craft. No funding rounds. No growth hacks. Just real problems, real solutions, and software that earns its place on your home screen.

Every app we ship starts from a place of genuine need. We were the frustrated fisherman who couldn't navigate WDFW regulations on mobile. We were the beer lover who wanted a real October challenge tracker. We were the parent who wanted more funny, whimsical picture books on the shelf — and ended up publishing a whole catalog of read-aloud stories for kids ages 2–10.

We build the apps we wished existed. Then we make them good enough for everyone.
